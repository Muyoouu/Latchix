# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in Latchix to be free of harassment and hostility. We
welcome people of any background, identity, experience level, and viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be direct and respectful. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good faith, but ask for evidence when a claim matters.
- Keep discussions on topic and actionable.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.
- Accept correction and repair harm when it happens.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, or sustained disruption.
- Discriminatory language or imagery.
- Sexual attention or advances.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern.

## Scope

This code applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews,
chat, events, and any space where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the project maintainer through the repository
hosting platform. Do not open a public issue about a conduct incident.

The maintainer may warn, remove content, restrict participation, or ban a
participant. Enforcement decisions are made in good faith and focus on the
safety of the community. Do not retaliate against a reporter.
